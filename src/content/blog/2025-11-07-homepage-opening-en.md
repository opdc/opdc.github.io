Hello, this is the Open Platform Developer Community (OPDC).

---

## Purpose of Website Launch

OPDC is a community established to enhance the productivity and quality of software developers related to open platforms, strengthen developer capabilities, promote mutual exchange, and develop and spread open source software.

Our website aims to:
- Provide news and information that serves as the foundation for community activities
- Create an open space where open platform developers can gather, learn, and grow together

---

## Our Commitment

OPDC will continue to:
- Work towards the development of the open platform ecosystem and the spread of open source
- Provide various educational programs, projects, and communication channels so that all developers can grow together

We look forward to your active participation and communication.
OPDC will always be with you!

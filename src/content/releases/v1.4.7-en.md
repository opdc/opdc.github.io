## Changes

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- **Language toggle URL fix**
  - Fixed a bug where switching to Korean from English leader detail pages (`/en/leaders/`) navigated to a non-existent page
  - The English slug suffix `-en` (e.g. `05-lee-kiha-en`) is now stripped to correctly resolve the Korean URL (`/leaders/05-lee-kiha/`)
  - Switching from Korean leader pages to English now also resolves the correct English URL

### ♻️ Code Improvements

- Added `src/lib/i18n.ts` utility: centralized slug transformation with `toKoSlug()` / `toEnSlug()`
  - Replaced 4 scattered inline `.replace(/-en$/, '')` calls across blog and leader pages

### 📝 Content

- Added current position for leader Kiha Lee: Adjunct Professor, IT Management, School of Business, Korea University of Technology and Education (KO/EN)
